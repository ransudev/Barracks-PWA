# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with React and TypeScript; delegated to the agent by the existing repository scaffold.

## Users

Primary users are barbers and front-desk staff working the shop floor and managing the day’s queue, bookings, customers, transactions, and stock. Secondary users are managers/administrators reviewing business performance and maintaining staff, barber, service, inventory, and system settings. A customer-facing account area supports profile, appointments, history, and loyalty information.

## Product Purpose

Barracks Barbers & Shaves is a desktop-first barbershop management PWA that brings the shop’s daily operations and management work into one shared interface. Success for this prototype means a reviewer can understand the role-based workflows and complete representative tasks using realistic mock data without a backend.

## Positioning

Inferred from the brief: the product connects the lived rhythm of a barbershop floor—queue, chair availability, bookings, service revenue, commissions, and stock—with management reporting in one brand-led workspace.

## Operating Context

Staff use the application during a working day at the front desk or shop floor under normal indoor lighting. Staff need fast scanning and low-friction updates while managers need a wider business-level picture. The old UI concept supplied with the brief is a functional reference for the navigation and page meanings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The frontend prototype should include a landing page, login, customer profile, staff dashboard, queue, bookings, customers, payment, inventory, staff settings, admin dashboard, staff management, barber management, services, reports, admin inventory, and admin settings. Use mock data and simulated local interactions only. Do not implement authentication, persistence, APIs, Supabase, databases, payments, or server-side business logic.

## Brand Commitments

The name Barracks Barbers & Shaves and its recognizable dark, masculine, premium barbershop character are binding. The attached PDF contains the existing Barracks logo treatment and functional UI concept; preserve the product meaning and familiar workflow while replacing the old visual design.

## Evidence on Hand

No production backend, real customer data, verified testimonials, or production imagery was provided; all displayed data in this prototype is synthetic and should be replaced before launch.

## Product Principles

- Keep the next operational action obvious.
- Separate staff operations from management oversight without splitting the brand.
- Let queue, bookings, services, and revenue tell one connected story.
- Make routine updates feel safe, reversible, and local.
- Keep the interface premium without sacrificing scanability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, clear status labels, and text labels alongside color-coded state. Avoid relying on color alone for queue, booking, payment, or stock status.
